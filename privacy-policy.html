FITme Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 4, 2026

FITme ("the app") is built privacy-first. The short version: your data never leaves your device.

Data We Collect

None. FITme has no user accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information.

Data Stored on Your Device

Workouts, meals, water intake, body measurements, and your profile settings are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. This data stays on your device (and in your personal device backups, which you control). Deleting the app deletes this data.

Apple Health (HealthKit)

With your explicit permission, FITme can:

  • Read steps, active energy, heart rate, body weight, walking/running distance, and workouts — solely to display your daily activity inside the app.
  • Write workouts and body weight that you log — so your Apple Health record stays complete.

Health data is accessed only on your device, is never transmitted anywhere, and is never used for marketing or shared with any third party. You can revoke access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

On-Device AI

AI features (coaching tips, workout suggestions, nutrition estimates) run entirely on your device using Apple's on-device foundation models. Your prompts and data are not sent to any external AI service.

Photos

The Med Scanner feature lets you pick a photo of a medicine label. The photo is analyzed on your device using Apple's Vision framework and is never uploaded or stored by the app.

Medical Information Disclaimer

FITme provides general wellness and educational information, including information about traditional Ayurvedic practices. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and is not a substitute for consultation with a qualified healthcare professional. Never stop or change prescribed medication based on information in the app.

Children

FITme is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone (it collects no information at all).
